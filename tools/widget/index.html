Klang on your Home Screen

Tomorrow's concerts on your Home Screen and Lock Screen. The listings and the widget itself update automatically.

  1. Install Scriptable from the App Store. It's free. scriptable.app
  2. Copy the widget script. You only do this once.
  3. Open Scriptable, tap +, and paste. Tap the name at the top, call it Klang, then tap Done. To check it, tap Klang in the list: a preview of the widget appears.
  4. On your Home Screen, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget. Choose Scriptable, pick a size and tap Add Widget.
  5. Touch and hold the new widget, tap Edit Widget, and set Script to Klang.
  6. To show the cities you chose in Klang: in Klang, open Me and tap Show my cities in the widget. Scriptable opens for a moment; then go back to Klang. Do this again whenever you change cities. Without this the widget shows all of Switzerland. A single widget can also show a country of its own: type Austria, Germany, France, Italy or All in its Parameter.
  7. For the Lock Screen (optional): touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen. Tap the widget area, choose Scriptable and add a widget. Tap it and set Script to Klang.
